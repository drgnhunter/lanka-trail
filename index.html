BEFORE YOU GO

Travel essentials

Keep these useful items in mind while preparing for your Sri Lankan adventure.

  • Lightweight clothing suitable for warm weather
  • Comfortable walking shoes and sandals
  • Sun protection, including sunscreen and a hat
  • A reusable water bottle
  • Any personal medication and travel documents
START EXPLORING

Plan your trip in 4 steps

  1. Choose your seasonCheck regional weather before selecting your dates.
  2. Pick your destinationsCombine beaches, hill country, heritage and wildlife.
  3. Plan your transportAllow enough time for travel between destinations.
  4. Prepare your essentialsOrganise entry documents, accommodation and packing.
A QUICK REFERENCE

When should you visit?

Weather varies across the island, so consider which coast or region you plan to explore.

RegionGenerally favourable periodPopular experiences
South & West CoastDecember – AprilBeaches, whale watching
East CoastMay – SeptemberSurfing, coastal escapes
Hill CountryJanuary – AprilHiking, tea country
Cultural TriangleJanuary – SeptemberHeritage, ancient sites

These are general travel-planning guidelines; local weather can vary.
